The confirmed product name is **Zawadi HR**. Use the full name in the primary logo lockup and marketing materials, with **ZAWADI HR** as the visual treatment. Use the standalone geometric emerald-and-navy mark only where space is limited, such as favicons, app icons, and compact navigation.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose a clean, broad HR brand rather than a payroll-only or services-oriented name, allowing the product to serve Tally and non-Tally customers.

**How to apply:** Keep the brand name as Zawadi HR across the app, public website, documents, and social materials. Do not add “Payroll System,” “Payroll Services,” or “Tally” to the core logo name. Describe the product separately as payroll, people management, and compliance software for Kenyan businesses.
